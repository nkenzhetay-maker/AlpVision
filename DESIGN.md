# AlpVision Design Reference — TRT Russian Editorial Covers

## Brand DNA

### Who we are
TRT Russian Dijital — Turkey's international public broadcaster's Russian-language digital arm. We serve Russian-speaking audiences worldwide with news from a non-Western, non-Russian editorial perspective.

### Visual identity
- **Primary color:** TRT Red `#B11731` — editorial authority, urgency
- **Secondary:** TRT Deep Blue `#042E58` — institutional trust, geopolitics
- **Accent:** TRT Cyan `#00B6CB` — digital modernity, neutrality
- **Supporting:** Mustard `#C48901` (features/analysis), Green `#216125` (security/military)
- **Neutrals:** Off-white `#F2F2F0`, Charcoal `#3A3A3A`, near-black `#1C1C1C` (never pure `#000`)
- **Logo:** "TRT НА РУССКОМ" — Manrope 900 + 500 weight, always top-left

### Typefaces
- **Display:** Oswald 700 — headlines, uppercase, tight leading (0.92–1.0)
- **Editorial serif:** Georgia / Times New Roman — for "By Invitation", analysis, opinion pieces
- **Body/UI:** Manrope 300–800 — everything else
- Never Inter. Never Roboto. These are the AI defaults.

## The Economist Lesson: Covers That Carry Arguments

### What makes Economist covers work
1. **Single visual metaphor** — one image encodes the entire editorial argument
2. **Layered symbolism** — objects work on dual registers (balloon = celebration AND threat)
3. **Scale distortion** — oversized objects signal disproportionate influence
4. **Color as argument** — red = danger/conflict, blue = order/technology, not decoration
5. **Anachronistic juxtaposition** — ancient + modern elements → cycles of history
6. **Weaponization parallels** — civilian objects placed next to military ones
7. **Instability indicators** — tilted figures, cracked symbols, downward trajectories

### What TRT Russian covers must do differently
- Economist is weekly, global, sardonic → we are daily, news-reactive, measured
- Economist uses custom illustration → we use AI-generated + editorial photography
- Economist is Western-centric irony → we offer the non-aligned perspective
- Our covers must work at Instagram thumbnail scale (200px) — Economist doesn't have this constraint

## Cover Design System — 30 Structural Families

Not 30 color variations. 30 genuinely different structural approaches:

### A. Photography-dominant (1–8)
1. **Full bleed photo** — photo fills frame, text in gradient overlay at bottom
2. **Photo + panel** — photo top, solid-color text panel bottom (3px accent border)
3. **Split** — vertical split: text left, photo right
4. **Photo + text top** — text block top, full photo bottom
5. **Collage** — B&W photo + red geometric overlays (conflict/tension)
6. **Portrait center** — person fills frame, text flanks sides
7. **Photo as texture** — heavily darkened/desaturated photo as background texture only
8. **Duotone photo** — photo processed to two-color (red+navy, cyan+black)

### B. Typography-dominant (9–16)
9. **Pure type dark** — black ground, Oswald headline fills 60% of frame
10. **Pure type light** — off-white ground, headline + thin accent line
11. **Quote card** — Georgia italic, large quote marks, attributed
12. **Data headline** — oversized number (67%, $3.2T) + context below
13. **Word stack** — 3–4 stacked words, different weights, creating rhythm
14. **Redacted** — visible text with strategic black bars (censorship theme)
15. **Breaking ticker** — red ground, white Oswald, modeled on TV lower-thirds
16. **Letter/invitation** — cream ground, red+blue envelope stripes, serif centered

### C. Illustration-concept (17–24)
17. **Metaphor center** — AI illustration dominates center, small text bottom
18. **Icon array** — grid of symbolic icons encoding the story's themes
19. **Map visual** — stylized map/geography + data overlay
20. **Silhouette** — stark silhouette figure on solid color ground
21. **Scale contrast** — tiny figure vs. massive object (power dynamic)
22. **Object study** — single symbolic object, clinical/editorial treatment
23. **Before/after** — split frame showing transformation
24. **Venn/overlap** — two concepts visually overlapping (collision metaphor)

### D. Hybrid/experimental (25–30)
25. **Magazine cover** — full masthead bar + cover photo (traditional magazine format)
26. **Tweet embed** — social media post card on dark background
27. **Geometric** — abstract shapes + type, constructivist influence
28. **Glitch/distortion** — photo with intentional digital artifacts (cyberwar, AI)
29. **Negative space** — most of frame is empty, subject tiny (isolation, void)
30. **Double exposure** — two images blended (connection between concepts)

## Typography Rules

### Headline hierarchy
- **Instagram (4:5):** Oswald clamp(1.4rem, 8.5vw, 4rem)
- **Twitter/Telegram (16:9):** Oswald clamp(1rem, 6vw, 4rem)
- **Web banner (3.2:1):** Oswald clamp(1rem, 3.6vw, 4rem)
- Line-height: 0.92 for uppercase Oswald, 1.15 for Manrope
- `text-wrap: balance` on all headlines

### Body/subtitle
- Manrope 400, clamp(0.48rem, 1.2vw, 0.72rem)
- 65–75ch max line length
- Italic for subtitles, weight 400

### Category labels
- Manrope 800, 0.22em letter-spacing, uppercase
- Color: accent (context-dependent)
- Size: clamp(0.3rem, 0.76vw, 0.46rem)

## Anti-Slop Rules for AlpVision

### Refuse by default
- Generic gradient backgrounds (purple→blue, pink→violet)
- Same template with just different accent colors ≠ "30 alternatives"
- Emoji as design elements in covers
- Stock illustration (Undraw, Humaaans style)
- Pure black `#000000` — always use `#1C1C1C` or darker tinted navy
- Card-in-card layouts
- Centered everything (headlines should be left-aligned or dramatically placed)
- "AI aesthetic" — geometric patterns for the sake of looking techy

### Require for every cover
- One defensible design choice per template (why THIS structure for THIS story?)
- Text readable at 200px thumbnail
- TRT НА РУССКОМ logo always present, always top-left
- Source/credit for photography when non-TRT
- Maximum 2 typefaces per cover (Oswald + Manrope, or Georgia + Manrope)

## Thumbnail-First Design

Every cover must survive the Instagram thumbnail test:
1. At 200px wide, can you read the main headline word?
2. At 200px wide, is the visual metaphor/subject recognizable?
3. At 200px wide, does the color scheme communicate the mood?

If any answer is no, increase contrast, simplify composition, or enlarge the key element.

## Platform Adaptation Rules

| Platform | Ratio | Key constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 4:5 (1080×1350) | Portrait — headline can be larger, more vertical space for photo |
| Twitter/X | 16:9 (1200×675) | Wide — headline must be punchier, less vertical stacking |
| Telegram | 16:9 (1280×720) | Same as Twitter but slightly larger |
| YouTube | 16:9 (1280×720) | Thumbnail must work at 120px height |
| Facebook | 1.91:1 (1200×628) | Ultrawide — horizontal composition only |
| Web/site | 3.2:1 (1920×600) | Banner — dramatic crop, minimal text |
